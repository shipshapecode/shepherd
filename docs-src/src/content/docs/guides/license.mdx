Shepherd.js is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/), with a commercial license for commercial use.

## Quick answers

**Is Shepherd.js free?** Yes, for personal and hobby projects, noncommercial open-source projects, education, and use by charities, public research organizations, and government institutions.

**Can I try it in a commercial project?** Yes. Any company can evaluate Shepherd.js for free for 30 consecutive days.

**Can I use Shepherd.js in a commercial or closed-source product?** Yes, with a commercial license. You never have to publish your source code.

**What does a commercial license cost?** A one-time payment with no recurring fees. The Business license is $50 for up to 5 projects. The Enterprise license is $300 for unlimited projects. [Buy a license](https://www.shepherdjs.dev/pricing).

**Does `react-shepherd` need a license too?** It has the same terms as `shepherd.js`. One Shepherd.js license covers both.

## Free use

The PolyForm Noncommercial License allows any noncommercial use at no cost. That includes:

- Personal projects, portfolios, hobby sites, and private study
- Open-source projects that have no anticipated commercial application
- Students, teachers, coursework, and academic research
- Charities, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental organizations, and government institutions, regardless of how they are funded

## Free evaluation

Any company may use Shepherd.js for any purpose for up to 30 consecutive days to decide whether it suits a project. Each company gets one evaluation period for all of its people. After 30 days, commercial use needs a commercial license.

## Commercial license

You need a commercial license when your use has an anticipated commercial application. For example:

- A commercial product, app, SaaS, or website that earns revenue
- Client work, agencies, and consulting projects
- Internal tools, dashboards, or admin panels at a for-profit company, even when they are not customer-facing
- Embedding Shepherd.js in a product you sell, resell, white-label, or distribute

A commercial license includes:

- Use in closed-source code, with no obligation to share your source
- Legal protection and indemnification
- Priority support and updates
- A lifetime license with no recurring fees

## Still not sure?

If your organization earns revenue and the project serves it, you need a commercial license. For anything else, email [ahoy@shipshape.io](mailto:ahoy@shipshape.io).

## Framework wrappers

- [React Shepherd](https://www.npmjs.com/package/react-shepherd) has the same license as `shepherd.js`.
- [Angular Shepherd](https://github.com/shipshapecode/angular-shepherd), [Vue Shepherd](https://github.com/shipshapecode/vue-shepherd), and [Ember Shepherd](https://github.com/RobbieTheWagner/ember-shepherd) depend on `shepherd.js`, so its license applies to any app that uses them.

## Earlier versions

Releases before this change were licensed under AGPL-3.0 (earlier releases under MIT). Those releases keep the license they were published under.
